# Klipp — City landmark shadow campaigns

## Brand principle

The primary Klipp logo remains the permanent, recognizable master identity. The city-specific landmark treatment is a **campaign layer**, never a replacement for the master logo.

## Creative idea

Use the existing shadow/negative-space silhouette language around the Klipp mark to introduce a subtle outline inspired by a recognizable landmark or architectural feature from the launch city. The landmark should feel discovered in the shadow, not pasted on as clip art.

## Rollout examples

- **Santo Domingo:** campaign silhouette inspired by a recognizable city landmark such as the Faro a Colón.
- **Santiago de los Caballeros:** use a locally meaningful landmark, selected after local creative review.
- **Future cities:** commission a distinct, locally researched silhouette for each launch market.

These are creative directions, not approved final artwork. Confirm the landmark, visual accuracy, cultural fit, and any permissions or usage restrictions before publishing.

## Where to use it

- City launch campaigns and localized paid media.
- Social posts, outdoor advertising, and promotional landing pages.
- Temporary welcome/onboarding moments tied to a city launch.
- Partner and professional acquisition campaigns in the launch city.

## Design guardrails

1. Keep the core Klipp symbol, wordmark, proportions, and brand colors intact.
2. Make the landmark a restrained shadow/negative-space detail; never let it overpower the mark.
3. Do not add literal palm trees, map pins, tools, or generic tourism clip art.
4. Create light and dark campaign variants with accessible contrast.
5. Use the city treatment only where the campaign context is clear; standard product UI and app icons retain the master identity.
6. Verify cultural accuracy and rights/permissions before using a recognizable protected design or commissioned artwork.
7. Build a reusable city campaign asset template so new cities can be added without redesigning the logo.

## Approval checklist

- [ ] Landmark selected and verified by local review.
- [ ] Shadow treatment remains legible at small sizes.
- [ ] Master Klipp logo is unchanged.
- [ ] Light/dark and social/ad formats exported.
- [ ] Rights and permissions checked.
- [ ] Campaign copy and city launch details reviewed.
